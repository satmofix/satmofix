Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective Date: 2026-11-22

1. Overview

Satmofix is a standalone GNSS/GPS coordinate and track recording tool. This app works fully offline and does NOT collect, upload, transmit, or share any user data to any server or third party.

2. Permission and Local Data

The app only requests precise location permission on your device for core functions:

  • Display real-time GNSS coordinates
  • Record local travel tracks
  • Provide positioning-related calculation

All location data, track records, and user-generated content are stored ONLY locally on your device. No data leaves your phone. No cloud upload, no background transmission, no data collection.

3. No Third-Party Services

This application:

  • Does not contain advertisements
  • Does not embed any third-party SDK
  • Does not collect device information, analytics, or user behavior data
  • Does not share any data with third parties

4. User Data Control

You may delete all local track data and app data at any time inside the application. We have no access to your local data.

5. Privacy Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Changes will be displayed on this page.

6. Contact

If you have any privacy-related questions, please contact:

一、概述

Satmofix 是一款纯本地 GNSS / GPS 坐标与轨迹记录工具。本应用完全离线运行，不会收集、上传、传输、分享任何用户数据至服务器或第三方平台。

二、权限与本地数据说明

本应用仅申请设备精准定位权限，用于实现核心功能：

  • 显示实时GNSS坐标
  • 记录本地轨迹路线
  • 完成定位相关计算

所有定位数据、轨迹记录、用户操作数据，仅保存在您的手机本地设备中。所有数据不会联网上传、不会后台传输、不会同步至云端。

三、无第三方数据收集

本应用：

  • 不含任何广告
  • 不含任何第三方统计、追踪、分析SDK
  • 不会收集设备信息、用户行为信息
  • 不会向任何第三方共享用户数据

四、用户数据控制权

您可在应用内随时手动删除全部轨迹数据与本地缓存数据。开发者无法查看、获取或管理您设备内的任何数据。
