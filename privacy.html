Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective Date: 2026-11-22

Overview

Satmofix is a GNSS positioning tool. It requests precise location permission to display GNSS coordinates, record waypoints and tracks.

The app may use network-assisted positioning (cell/Wi‑Fi) for faster location acquisition. This is provided by your device system.

All your location data, waypoints and track records are stored locally on your device only. They will never be uploaded to remote servers, sent to cloud services, or shared with any third parties.

Data stored locally

  • GNSS coordinate information
  • Waypoints (marked points)
  • Track path records

Permissions explanation

  • Location: Required to read GNSS, record waypoints and tracks.
  • Network: Only for system-assisted positioning, we do not collect or upload your location data.

No user accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics tracking.

生效日期：2026-11-22

概述

Satmofix 是GNSS定位工具，需要精准定位权限，用来显示GNSS坐标、记录航点（打点）和轨迹。

应用可以使用网络辅助定位（基站/WiFi）加快定位锁定速度，该能力由设备系统提供。

你的所有位置数据、航点、轨迹记录仅保存在本机，不会上传到远程服务器、云端，也不会分享给任何第三方。

本地存储内容

  • GNSS坐标信息
  • 航点（打点标记）
  • 轨迹路径记录

权限说明

  • 位置权限：读取GNSS、记录航点和轨迹必需
  • 网络权限：仅用于系统辅助定位，我们不会收集、上传你的位置信息

无账号系统、无云端同步、无数据统计追踪。
